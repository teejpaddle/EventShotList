Nikon quick reference

Starting settings for event volunteers. Menu names from the Nikon Z6III manual; your model may differ.

Photo: set once

Shooting mode
A (Aperture-priority auto)
ISO
Auto ISO, highest ISO about 12800
Minimum shutter speed
1/250 s (1/125 s for a seated audience)
Focus mode
AF-C (Continuous AF)
Focus area
Wide-area AF (L)
Face and eye detection
AF/MF subject detection options: People
White balance
Keep overall atmosphere
File format
RAW + JPEG fine★
Drive mode (burst)
Continuous L
Exposure compensation (+/−)
0, then adjust in steps of 0.3

Photo: by situation

Indoor hall or sabha
f/2.8 to f/4 · 1/125 s for seated people, 1/250 s for people moving · Auto, up to 12800
Stage performance
As wide as your lens allows (f/2.8 or lower) · 1/500 s for dance, 1/250 s for speakers · Auto, up to 12800 (25600 if needed) · −0.7 (dark stages make the camera overexpose faces)
Low-light arti and diya moments
Widest your lens allows (f/1.8 to f/2.8) · 1/125 s (1/60 s if the person is still and you brace yourself) · Auto, up to 25600 · −0.3 to −0.7, so the flames keep their shape
Outdoor daylight (e.g. Walkathon)
f/5.6 to f/8 · 1/500 s · Auto (will stay low, around 100 to 400)
Group photos
f/5.6 to f/8, so every row is sharp · 1/200 s · Auto

Ask the event lead before using flash. No flash during prayers or arti unless allowed.

Video: set once

Frame rate
30p (29.97)
Shutter speed
1/60 s at 30p · 1/125 s at 60p
Exposure mode
M (Manual), with auto ISO
Resolution and format
3840×2160; 30p, video file type H.264 8-bit (MP4)
Picture profile
Set Picture Control: Standard
White balance
Fixed, not Auto. Indoors: about 3200 to 4000 K. Outdoors: Daylight.
Autofocus
AF-F (Full-time AF), AF/MF subject detection: People
Audio level
External mic, loudest sounds peaking around −12 dB
Headphones
Plugged in and listening the whole time
Stabilization
Vibration reduction on. Electronic VR: ON when walking, OFF on a tripod

Video: by situation

Stage program
Tripod · 4K 30p (1080p 30p for very long programs) · 1/60 s · f/2.8 to f/4
Walking or outdoor event
Handheld, or a gimbal if you have one · 4K 30p · 1/60 s with an ND filter; without one, up to 1/250 s is fine · f/5.6 to f/8
Interviews and testimonials
Tripod at the person’s eye level · 4K or 1080p, 30p · 1/60 s · f/2.8 to f/4, for a soft background
B-roll
4K 60p, so it can be slowed down · 1/125 s · f/2.8 to f/5.6

Bring 3 charged batteries and a spare card. Wear headphones.
