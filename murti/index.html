Murti

Reference shots of each murti in the mandir. Shoot straight on, level, and without flash.

  • Stand centered and hold the camera level with the murtis, so the frame and pillars look straight.
  • No flash. Use the low-light settings if the mandir is dim.
  • Use f/5.6 to f/8 so every murti in the frame is sharp, and brace yourself or lean on a wall.
  • Take one shot of the full shringar, then closer details.
